## Reading

Ids are disjoint by construction, but ids are not the leakage unit for neuron search: the pressure search and scored sets are drawn from the same two templates, so a neuron found on `spine_contrast` is a template-level feature and any claim about it needs a template-disjoint split (or a held-out template) before it counts. The smoke-run search in `raw.json` is worse still (it used the scored items themselves) and is labelled EXPLORATORY.
